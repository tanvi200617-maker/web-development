About Me

I am a passionate and curious technology student at World College of Technology and Management** with a strong interest in exploring how technology can be used to solve real-world problems. I enjoy learning new technologies, experimenting with different tools, and turning ideas into practical and creative projects. My areas of interest include **web development, Python, SQL, machine learning, and digital design. I believe that technology is not just about writing code, but also about understanding problems, finding effective solutions, and creating experiences that are useful and meaningful.

Beyond academics, I actively participate in my college's technology community. I am a Core Team Member of AWS Campus Ambassador and a **Community Member of The Codex Society**. Through these experiences, I get opportunities to collaborate with other students, participate in technical activities, contribute to college events, and explore new ideas. Working with different people has helped me improve my teamwork, communication, leadership, creativity, and problem-solving skills** while giving me practical experience outside the classroom.

I am someone who enjoys learning by doing. Whether I am developing a website, exploring a programming concept, working with data, or experimenting with a new design, I like understanding how things work and finding ways to make them better. I am continuously working on improving my technical and creative skills through projects, practice, and new experiences. I believe that every project, challenge, and collaboration is an opportunity to learn something new, and I look forward to building more innovative and meaningful work as I continue my journey in technology.
